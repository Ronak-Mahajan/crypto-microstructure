## Reading it

(a) is the sanity anchor: if the book rebuild and OFI are right, the contemporaneous R^2 at 10 s should be large. (b) is the question: an OOS R^2 whose CI excludes zero at a horizon is predictability at that horizon. (c) is the answer that matters: edge minus cost. (d) is only measurable on self-recorded days.
